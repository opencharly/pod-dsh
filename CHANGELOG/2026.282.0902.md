# 2026.282.0902 — chore(dsh-web): drop the @perrylink/dsh-github and dsh-workspace-enhancement community plugins

## Summary

Drops two of the four community/native plugins from the `dsh-web` web profile, so it composes **eight** bundles (was ten). Closes #31. Head: `deef87a4942c8e4aee08a11278f5366f80a1fa9e`.

| Dropped bundle | Why |
|---|---|
| `dsh-workspace-enhancement` ("Remote workspaces") | Operator-requested removal (2026-10-09). |
| `@perrylink/dsh-github` | Not needed: nothing in `AGENTS.md`, the marketplace corpus or any `check:` step references its tools (`pr_create`, `issue_open`, `gh_review`, `gh_checks`, and friends); the rulebook's interface is the `gh` CLI; its entire write surface is approval-gated and therefore auto-rejected whenever approvals are disabled (the umbrella ledgers record the `issue_open` rejected → `gh issue create` works fallback); and its polling review bot + status-check gate duplicate the org's authoritative `charly/pr-validator`. |

What the cutover carries:

| Surface | Change |
|---|---|
| `candy/dsh-web/web-profile/package.json` | both deps and both `dsh.profile.bundles` entries removed (10 → 8 bundles) |
| `candy/dsh-web/web-profile/vendor-tgz/` | both tarballs deleted; `SHA256SUMS` 4 → 2; `README.md` rows + the ssh2 non-reproducibility section removed |
| `candy/dsh-web/web-profile/pnpm-workspace.yaml` | `allowBuilds` (ssh2, cpu-features) and `patchedDependencies` removed — dead with ssh2 |
| `candy/dsh-web/web-profile/patches/ssh2@1.17.0.patch` | deleted (only consumer was ssh2) |
| `candy/dsh-web/charly.yml` | `gcc`/`gcc-c++`/`make`/`python3` and the `node-gyp` install step removed (nothing compiles now: both remaining plugins are prebuilt); the three profile checks and the `description:` retitled; a negative assertion added so a revert fails |
| `charly.yml` | the `dsh-skill:` prose (plugin table, bundle counts) and the `check-dsh-pod` seeded assertion updated the same way |

The two remaining plugins (`dsh-git-worktree`, `dsh-opencharly`) stay vendored as prebuilt `file:` tarballs. Corpus regeneration is owned by the marketplace's daily `refresh.yml` (it advances the `opencharly/pod-dsh` ref and regenerates the `/charly-tools:dsh` SKILL.md from this repo's `skill:` entity), so no marketplace PR is part of this cutover.
